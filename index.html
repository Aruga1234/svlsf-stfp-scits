About the SoftTech Fellowship

A comprehensive industry-aligned skill development & placement-oriented programme

The SoftTech Fellowship is a flagship initiative designed exclusively for technology students and recent graduates. It bridges the gap between academic learning and real-world industry requirements in software engineering, emerging technologies, and digital product development.

Spanning 16–24 weeks, the programme combines rigorous technical training, real-world project experience, structured mentorship by industry practitioners, and dedicated placement assistance.

CPA Accreditation is applicable to this fellowship, enhancing the professional value of the certification for candidates.

Key Highlights

  • Industry-aligned curriculum
  • Hands-on Capstone Projects
  • Mentorship by Industry Experts
  • Soft Skills & Interview Preparation
  • Need-based Stipend Support
  • 100% Placement Assistance
  • Joint Certification + CPA Accreditation
